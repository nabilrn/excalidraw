THE WORKSPACE

Move from “what do I need to do?” to “how do I solve it?” without leaving the app.

01

Pick a task. Start the clock.

The Tasks view keeps your active task, estimated duration, linked canvases, monthly completion calendar, and focus rail on one screen.

  • Compact task sidebar
  • Pause, finish, or clear a focus session
  • Session history per task
  • Handwritten monthly completion markers
02

Open the thinking space behind the task.

Canvases are organized into task-backed groups automatically. Open one and you get the full Excalidraw editor for diagrams, flows, notes, and rough problem solving.

  • Task-backed groups created automatically
  • Manual groups for loose ideas
  • Search across canvases
  • Full Excalidraw editing experience

LOCAL-FIRST + OPTIONAL SYNC

Use it offline. Back it up when you want.

FocusCanvas does not require an account to work. Your live workspace stays on your machine. Google Drive sync is an optional backup layer for recovery and moving to another device.

Private Drive storage FocusCanvas uses Google Drive's appDataFolder scope. The backup is hidden from normal My Drive files and is only used by the app.
